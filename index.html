Subscriptions

Breathwork Pro starts with a free month, then renews monthly or yearly. To cancel or change your plan, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or Settings › Manage subscription inside the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

New phone? Tap Restore purchases on the paywall or in Settings. Have a promo code? Tap Have a promo code? on the paywall.

Safety

Breathwork is a training aid, not medical advice. Only practise breath holds on dry land, sitting or lying down, and never in or near water.
