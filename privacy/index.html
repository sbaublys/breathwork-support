Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Breathwork does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No accounts. You never sign up or sign in.
  • On-device only. Your training sessions, max holds and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No permissions. The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data.
  • Subscriptions. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Safety

Breathwork is a training aid, not medical advice. Only practise breath-hold training on dry land, sitting or lying down, and never in or near water.
